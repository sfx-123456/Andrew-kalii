ANDREWMOLE — KALI ROOM BOOKING WEBSITE
========================================

Included:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js
- images/ (four uploaded reference photos)

PRICES:
- 30 minutes: ₹300
- 1 hour: ₹1,000
- Non-AC room: no extra charge
- AC room: +₹200

BOOKING FLOW:
Customers enter their name, duration, room type, preferred date and start time, number of guests, and optional requests. The form calculates the estimated total and opens WhatsApp with a message addressed to 7012742170. Andrewmole must confirm availability; submitting the form does not itself confirm a reservation.

OPEN IN SPCK EDITOR:
1. Download and extract this ZIP on your phone.
2. Open Spck Editor and open the extracted folder as a project.
3. Open index.html and tap Preview/Run.

The photos are stored locally in the images folder. Google Fonts requires an internet connection; the site can use fallback fonts if offline.
